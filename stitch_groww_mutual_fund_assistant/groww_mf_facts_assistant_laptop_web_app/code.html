What is the mandatory lock-in period and minimum SIP amount for HDFC ELSS Taxsaver Fund?
G

HDFC ELSS Taxsaver Fund has a mandatory statutory lock-in period of 3 years from the allotment date of each unit, under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act. The minimum Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) amount specified by the scheme is ₹500 (and in multiples of ₹500 thereafter).

Source: HDFC ELSS SID Oct 2024 (Section II, Page 14)
