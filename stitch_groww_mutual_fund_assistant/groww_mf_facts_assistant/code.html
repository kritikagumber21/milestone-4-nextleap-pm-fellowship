What is the lock-in period and minimum SIP amount for HDFC ELSS Taxsaver Fund?
check

HDFC ELSS Taxsaver Fund has a statutory mandatory lock-in period of 3 years (36 months) from the date of allotment under Section 80C. The minimum SIP investment is ₹500 per installment (and in multiples of ₹500 thereafter). Units cannot be redeemed, transferred, or pledged during the lock-in tenure.

What is the current expense ratio and exit load for HDFC Flexi Cap Direct Plan?
check

As per latest monthly disclosures, the Total Expense Ratio (TER) for HDFC Flexi Cap Direct Plan is 0.78% (Regular Plan: 1.54%). An exit load of 1.00% is payable if redeemed or switched out within 1 year from the date of allotment; no exit load applies after 1 year.
